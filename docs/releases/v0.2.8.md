# eStream Assistant v0.2.8 (test build)

The Pending tab is easier to work through, and the ? help explains every tab and has a search.

## New
- **The row being asked about is highlighted on the receipt** even for receipts Claude read as a PDF (like the
  Costco receipts saved with 0.2.6 or earlier). The app finds the rows on the picture itself with Windows' own
  text reader: nothing is sent to Claude.
- **Zoom and move the receipt with the mouse** on the Pending tab: the wheel zooms in and out where the pointer is,
  double-click zooms in (double-click again to go back), and holding and dragging moves the receipt.
  Use drag or the scroll bar to go along a long receipt.
- **Help (?)**: a new "The tabs" tab says in very simple words what each tab is for, and a search at the top
  finds the right boxes on both help tabs (try "stock sheet" or "check inventory counts"; spelling slips are fine).

## Changed
- "Finish" is now **Done** on the Pending tab. The Back and Later buttons are the same size.
- Claude answers questions about the app in a few plain sentences and points to the ? help for more.

## Install
Download **eStreamAssistant-Setup-0.2.8.exe** below and run it (no admin rights needed). Windows may show
*"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info → Run anyway**. Updating keeps settings, the saved API key,
remembered picks, and receipts saved for later. Needs Windows 10 (version 1809) or later.
